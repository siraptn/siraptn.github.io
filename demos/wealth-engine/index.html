Wealth Engine: messy inputs in, clean records out

An AI data-entry agent reads a receipt, a bank statement or an exchange API, proposes structured rows, and writes nothing until you approve. Approved rows land in the ledger and the dashboard updates.

How this demo works: it replays the real agent’s flow on made-up data, in your browser. The production version runs privately on Claude with Google Sheets and Cloudflare, behind Cloudflare Access.
