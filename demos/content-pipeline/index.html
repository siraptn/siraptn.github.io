1. Sample input talk script excerpt

Most AI pilots in Southeast Asia do not fail on the model. They fail on the hand-off. A team proves a chatbot works in four weeks, then nobody owns the data, the approvals or the Monday-morning habit. Six months later it is a slide. What worked for us was boring: one owner per workflow, a human approval step before anything writes to a system, and a weekly number the business already cares about. In my own businesses I run agents the same way. They draft, I approve. They never touch money or customers without me. That rule is why I trust them with more every month. The question I ask every client now is not "which model?" It is "who approves, and what number moves?"
