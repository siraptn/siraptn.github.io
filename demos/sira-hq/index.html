Sira HQ: one inbox, every business

Messages and notes arrive from LINE, email, Telegram and social chats. The agent works out which business it belongs to, picks the workflow, and proposes the tool calls (calendar, email, sheets) for you to approve. Ask for a brief and it summarises every business at once.

How this demo works: it replays the agent’s routing on sample messages, in your browser. The production version is a Claude Agent Skill with Google Workspace as the system of record through MCP connectors. Channel capture is shown as designed and is wired up one channel at a time.
