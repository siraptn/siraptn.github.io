AI search engine: found by buyers, cited by AI

Built for Kiren BBQ, a restaurant engineering business. Every week the agent checks the questions B2B buyers ask, sees whether the catalog shows up in Google and in AI answers, and proposes fixes for the gaps.

How this demo works: it replays a weekly run in your browser. Product names, positions and scores are illustrative samples, not live results. The production version runs on Claude against a product database in Google Sheets.
